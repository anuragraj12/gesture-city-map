DEMO

Fly the city with your hands.

Every star is a place worth knowing, and every category is its own constellation. Hand tracking runs entirely in your browser, so no video ever leaves your device.

✋ ✥
Four fingers togetherHold four fingers side by side and move your hand. The map follows, in any direction.
✌️ ↕
Two fingers up · downShow index and middle finger. Move up to zoom in, down to zoom out.
☝️
One fingerPoint to move the pointer. It snaps onto the nearest place.
👇
Fold your fingerWhile pointing, fold the index finger and lift it again to open the place.
✊
FistHold for a moment to close the open place.
🙌
Two handsShow both hands and turn them like a steering wheel to rotate.

Works best in Chrome or Edge, in good light, with your hand 40–70 cm from the camera. Pins are approximate.

Gestures and keys

🖐️
Spread palm = restFingers spread apart do nothing. Close them together to grab the map, spread them to let go.
🎯
Click lands where you pointedThe pointer freezes the moment your finger starts to fold. Fold and lift within half a second; hold it folded and it becomes a fist.
⏱
Holds, not flicksFist (close) and thumbs up (also opens) fire after about a third of a second, once per gesture.
🧠
Train your ownPress 🧠 Train (T), record a pose for two seconds and pick what it does. It saves in your browser.
🙌
Two hands winWhile both hands are in view, only rotation works.
⏸
Pause any timePress Space or the ⏸ button.
🏆
ExploreTick off the top places in each category, earn points and badges, and share your constellation.

🧠 Train gestures

Teach the map your own hand shapes. Name a gesture, pick what it does, then record it for two seconds.

Camera offRecording turns it on

    Tips: while recording, move your hand a little nearer, further and tilted so it learns the pose, not one position. If two gestures get mixed up, record more of each, or add an Ignore gesture for the shape it should leave alone. Trained gestures win over the built-in ones when they match.
